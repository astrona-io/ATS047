# Summary

An SLI measures a defined service outcome, an SLO sets its target over a window, and an SLA adds an agreement with its own consequences. Good and valid event definitions determine the population.

The error budget represents allowed unacceptable outcomes under that objective. Burn rate and remaining budget support operational decisions, but missing evidence, low traffic and exclusions must be stated rather than hidden in reassuring percentages.
